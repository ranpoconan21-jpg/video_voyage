Videos Voyage

Videos Voyage is a personal tool that creates short travel videos from public data (cheapest flight prices from Paris, weather averages) and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

The tool is used only by its owner to publish to their own accounts. It does not offer accounts or services to other users.
