Privacy Policy

Videos Voyage is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

1. Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the tool receives OAuth access tokens from Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) and TikTok, plus basic profile information needed to publish (account ID, username). It uses the permissions to upload videos and read publishing status.

2. How data is used

Tokens are used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's accounts. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sent anywhere other than the platform that issued them. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google, Meta or TikTok account settings, and the local files are then deleted.

4. Viewers

The tool does not collect any data about people who watch the videos.

5. Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
